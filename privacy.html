Privacy notice

Last updated: 19 October 2026.

Who is responsible?

Khan Group CommV, trading as Belgium Ride, is the controller of personal data used to manage your journey. Registered address: Hallebaan 10, 9450 Haaltert, Belgium. VAT BE1001463731. Contact: info@belgiumride.com, +32 466 19 08 24.

Information we use and why

We use your name, email, telephone number, pickup and destination addresses, journey date and time, passenger and luggage counts, selected service, flight number and instructions you provide to calculate quotes, assess requests, arrange transport and communicate with you. This is necessary to take steps at your request before entering into a contract and to perform that contract (GDPR Article 6(1)(b)). Required contact and journey information is needed to process a booking; without it we cannot arrange your ride.

We use booking references, payment and refund identifiers, amounts, statuses and relevant correspondence for payment administration and accounting. Our legal bases are performance of the contract and compliance with applicable accounting and tax obligations (Article 6(1)(c)). Security logs and relevant records may also be used to prevent fraud, investigate incidents and handle or defend claims, relying on our legitimate interests in protecting customers, our service and legal rights (Article 6(1)(f)).

Optional notes should contain only practical journey information. Do not enter payment card details, identity documents or medical information in the booking form. Contact us separately about assistance requirements without sending unnecessary health details. Booking is not consent to marketing.

Who receives information?

Access is limited to people arranging your ride and providers needed to operate the service: Supabase for booking records and driver authentication; Stripe for payments and refunds; Resend for booking emails; Hostinger for our business mailbox; GitHub Pages for website hosting; and Google Maps Platform for address search and route calculations. Google receives relevant addresses and technical information when its features are used. Stripe collects card details on its own checkout; our booking database stores payment references and statuses rather than full card numbers or security codes.

Website delivery also involves external content providers, including the JavaScript CDN and image hosts used by the page. Your browser sends technical information such as IP address and browser details when requesting external resources. If you contact us using another service, such as WhatsApp, that service also processes information under its own privacy terms. We may disclose necessary records to accountants, professional advisers or competent authorities where justified or legally required.

International processing

Some providers operate internationally and may process data outside the European Economic Area. Relevant transfers must be covered by an applicable adequacy decision or appropriate safeguards, such as European Commission standard contractual clauses. Contact info@belgiumride.com for details of the transfer arrangements applicable to your information and how to obtain a copy of the relevant safeguards.

How long information is kept

Retention depends on the purpose and the applicable legal obligations. Uncompleted quotes and unsuccessful requests should be retained only while needed to process the request, resolve payment issues or investigate misuse. Operational journey details are kept only while needed to deliver the service and resolve related queries or disputes. Accounting and payment evidence is retained for the statutory period applicable to the record. Records needed for an actual or reasonably anticipated legal claim may be kept until that matter and the applicable limitation period have ended. These criteria also apply to email copies and provider-held records; deletion from a booking screen does not necessarily erase accounting evidence or backups immediately.

Your rights

Depending on the circumstances, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of your data and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Where processing relies on consent, you may withdraw that consent without affecting earlier lawful processing. Some records cannot be erased while a legal retention obligation or justified legal claim applies.

Send requests to info@belgiumride.com. We may request proportionate information to verify your identity. We normally respond within one month; where a lawful extension is necessary, we will explain it. You may complain to the Belgian Data Protection Authority at www.dataprotectionauthority.be or another competent supervisory authority.

Browser storage and decisions

The booking flow uses session storage to remember your booking reference during a browser session. The driver portal uses authentication storage to keep authorised users signed in. Third-party services may use their own storage as described in their privacy information. We do not require agreement to advertising for you to request a ride. Quotes and available times are calculated automatically from route, pricing and availability information; final ride approval is made by the driver.

Contact and updates

Contact info@belgiumride.com with privacy questions. Material changes to how information is used will be reflected in this notice and communicated where required.
