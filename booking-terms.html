Booking, cancellation and waiting terms

Belgium Ride is operated by Khan Group CommV, Hallebaan 10, 9450 Haaltert, Belgium. VAT: BE1001463731. Contact: info@belgiumride.com · +32 466 19 08 24.

Booking and payment

Review your journey, pickup date, Belgian local pickup time and total fare before payment. Payment does not itself confirm your ride. Your ride is confirmed only when the driver approves it and you receive confirmation. If we decline or cancel your ride, we provide a full refund to the original payment method.

Payment is collected when you complete checkout. Your ride requires driver approval. If we decline or cancel your ride, you receive a full refund.

All-inclusive transport fares

The fare shown before payment is the total transport price for the agreed journey, including applicable VAT, normal parking and tolls. There are no hidden booking fees. Additional waiting beyond the free allowance, extra stops or customer-requested itinerary changes are outside the agreed journey and require your agreement to any additional price before they are provided.

Customer cancellation

Cancel at least 24 hours before the agreed pickup time for a full refund. Cancellations received less than 24 hours before pickup are not refundable. Contact info@belgiumride.com or +32 466 19 08 24 and provide your booking reference. The time your request reaches us determines the cancellation deadline. Keep a copy of your request. This policy does not limit mandatory consumer rights.

Refunds are returned to the original payment method. Bank processing times vary; a cancellation acknowledgment does not mean your bank has already credited the refund.

Included waiting

  • Airport pickups: 60 minutes from your flight's actual arrival time. Provide your flight number when booking and contact us if you cannot find your driver.
  • City and other pickups: 15 minutes from the agreed pickup time, provided the driver is present and ready.

After the included period, additional waiting costs €10 per started 15 minutes (€40 per hour). We will inform you and obtain your agreement before paid additional waiting begins. For example, 20 additional minutes cost €20. Additional waiting is arranged and paid separately; the initial Stripe payment is not automatically increased. Extra waiting is subject to driver availability. Time caused by our driver's delay is not charged to you.

Private day trips

Each listed day trip includes four hours at the destination. Outward and return travel time is additional to those four hours. Confirm your pickup and return arrangements with the driver. Admission tickets, meals and extra activities are not promised as part of the transport service; ask us before booking if you need specific arrangements. Changes or additional services require agreement on any extra price before they are provided.

Journey details and assistance

Check your contact details, addresses, flight information, passenger and luggage counts before paying. Tell us about child seats, accessibility requirements or unusual luggage before booking so we can confirm whether we can accommodate them. Contact us promptly for changes, complaints or assistance.
